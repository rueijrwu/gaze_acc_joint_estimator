# Full-information experiment protocol

This workflow diagnoses calibration information. Protocol gaze and demand labels are targets, not independent measurements of actual eye state. The existing recordings therefore support conditional consistency, stability and identifiability checks; physiological accuracy remains not evaluable without independent references.

The current-solution report separates optical residual loss, gaze and accommodation anchors, temporal penalties and function/model priors. Reconstructing the seven frozen solutions gave original-objective differences no larger than `1e-15`; all fourteen model/checkpoint hashes matched before and after the diagnostic. These solutions use different training cohorts and sometimes different model capacities, so their total costs do not rank models. Fixed-coefficient noise propagation is conditional on the fitted optical function and omits coefficient uncertainty.

Mean probes use frozen H0 and FC0 solutions, their supported demand-2 center fixation 17, and requested shifts of `-0.5` and `+0.5` degrees from each solution's fitted mean. A separately recorded quadratic probe induces the shift while all remaining states and optical coefficients are reoptimized. The horizontal coordinate is the achieved mean. The reported constrained stationarity removes only the selected fixation's uniform gaze multiplier from the original objective and respects state bounds. These are local stationary profiles, not exact requested-mean solutions, global minima or confidence intervals. Each run has a 900-second cap and unchanged physical stopping criteria; incomplete runs remain explicitly incomplete.

The matched internal-validation design holds out the entire fixation 17. Its three-demand training cohort also excludes fixation indices 10–14; its four-demand cohort adds those five demand-3 fixations. Both use the same 14-by-12 coefficient map derived from demand knots approximately `0.36036, 2, 4`, so the demand-3 optical coefficient is interpolated between 2 and 4 in both arms. Exponent, noise policy, prior function, prior ranges and regularization are frozen from the common three-demand training cohort. Fresh training-only initialization avoids using a frozen solution that trained on the withheld fixation.

The normalized observations are `[d, rho] = [m/S1, S4/S1]`. The full relative observations are `[d, rho, log(S1)]`, an invertible transform of the ordered positive pixel basis `[m, S1, S4]`. The correlated covariance is a delta-method approximation at a training-only anchor. Consecutive-frame variation also includes motion and detector variation; it is not calibrated localization noise.

Fixed scale and finite log-scale uncertainty are explicit conditional hypotheses. A full-relative arm adds three low-order log-shape coefficients, with a separately reported common training-only function prior. Finite uncertainty inflates only the third covariance diagonal and uses an elliptical radial robust residual model; it does not profile a separate Gaussian scale prior under the robust loss. Free framewise scale eliminates the third observation exactly and drops the log-shape prior, recovering the matched normalized objective. Costs across fixed, uncertain and free hypotheses are not comparable likelihoods or model-selection scores: covariance inflation has no log-determinant term and the prior dimensions differ. No scale assumption is selected by its total cost.

An external validation contract requires randomized repeated recordings, immutable cohort/source identities, independent gaze and accommodation references with uncertainty and alignment, withheld recording identities, declared coverage/failure handling, head-condition strata, and success thresholds fixed before evaluation. Missing independent references or thresholds must fail closed as `not_evaluable_without_independent_references`; nominal target agreement never substitutes for independent accuracy.
