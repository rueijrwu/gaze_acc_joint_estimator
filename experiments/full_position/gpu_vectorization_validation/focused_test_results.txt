Focused GPU profile / device LSMR checks
========================================

Profile and fit cases: 8 passed in the full focused run. These cover the four
response exponents for CPU/GPU update fields, QR sign-invariant R parity,
captured JVP/VJP parity and immutability, plus converged and max_nfev=1 fits.

LSMR case: the initial run failed because the test RHS omitted the nine zero
penalty rows from the augmented regularized operator. After correcting that
fixture, CPU/GPU LSMR diagnostics differed around a near-breakdown stopping
threshold (including an 11 vs 12 iteration result); that comparison was
restructured to compare exact fixed-five-iteration recurrences with stopping
tolerances disabled, and converged answers via solution, recomputed residual,
and normal-equation gradient. The corrected standalone LSMR case passed (1
passed). The eight profile/fit cases were not rerun after the LSMR-only change.

HostView guard follow-up: 1 targeted test passed, confirming the lazily copied
NumPy cache and slices reject writes. The production change only sets the
cached host array read-only; the GPU profile algebra is unchanged.

Benchmark controls and timings are recorded in profile_gpu_benchmark.json.
The benchmark used maxiter=200 with zero stopping tolerances; it recorded the
budget and wall time, not returned LSMR iteration counts. Timings are operator
measurements and do not represent full-fit runtime.
