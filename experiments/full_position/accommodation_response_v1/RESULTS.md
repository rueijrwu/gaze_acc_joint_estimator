# Accommodation response law: sampled development screen

**Status:** Complete sampled development screen; not nested model selection. No exponent was selected or promoted. The logarithmic conditional27 xy response remains the reference.

The four fixed 27-coefficient laws were compared with the same geometry, xy information, fold-local log27 pilot and covariance, per-frame two-state calibration, soft mean anchors (0.10 deg, 0.25 D), column-normalized prior (0.001), 49-start scalar inverse, and central-80% sampling (48 training and eight evaluation rows per fixation). Gaze folds withheld one horizontal target across captures; capture folds withheld each complete capture. There were nine folds and 36 candidate fits. All 36 fits certified in 246.13 seconds using 12 CPU workers and one BLAS/OMP thread each. Each candidate had 143/160 complete evaluation frames and 429/480 scored P4 slots; all expected exposures contributed. The 51 unavailable slots per family remain explicit. No candidate-specific pilot, covariance, or reference-state differences were found within any fold.

The primary comparison is paired equal-exposure excluded-P4 squared error, `L_cross`; negative `ΔL` favors the candidate. The table reports RMS summaries in pixels and physical subset-state disagreement in degrees and diopters. These are descriptive comparisons, not acceptance limits.

The exact paired `ΔL` values show why no law wins across split families. On all 143 complete gaze frames, shifted quadratic improves `L_cross` by 0.389 px², while its gaze disagreement rises by 0.0166 deg² and accommodation disagreement by 0.0068 D². Its gain is not stable across support: on the jointly empirical-state-and-P1 cohort (63 frames), `ΔL` is +0.357 px². Shifted sqrt and linear worsen full-cohort gaze `L_cross` by 0.679 and 1.270 px². On capture frames, linear has the largest full-cohort gain (`ΔL = -1.936 px²`), but it becomes +0.121 px² on jointly interior support (111 frames) and +0.0145 px² on joint state-and-P1 support (85 frames); bound slots rise from 8 under log to 67 under linear. Capture shifted quadratic improves full-cohort `L_cross` by 0.931 px² while increasing state disagreement by 0.246 deg² and 0.259 D², with 96 bound slots.

These are descriptive, candidate-dependent cohorts. They do not show that bounds or support filtering caused any gain. All five paired cohorts, including their frame and point counts, are in `summary.json`; exact memberships are in `paired_memberships.jsonl.gz`. `exposure_group_deltas.csv` records paired per-exposure changes in E², G theta², G A² and worst-point², plus signed-axis means, axis RMS, tails, coverage, bounds and support counts. `training_trajectory_diagnostics.csv` separately records each training fixation's nominal mean offset, temporal spread and nominal RMS. The identity decomposing nominal RMS into mean offset and temporal spread holds to saved floating-point precision. Nominal offsets and temporal spread are descriptive calibration diagnostics, not framewise accuracy measurements.

Training-only accommodation scale diagnostics show increasing reparameterization as the response departs from log: fitted positive gains on `1+A` range 1.040–1.093 for shifted sqrt, 1.012–1.154 for linear and 0.989–1.304 for shifted quadratic. The associated residual trajectory RMS also increases. These diagnostics were not applied to evaluation states and do not establish a physiological lens exponent. The column-normalized prior has the same numeric strength across candidates but is not equivalent regularization in function space.

## Interpretation and scope

No law wins across both split families and support cohorts. Keep fresh log27 as the reference. This historical sampled screen is development evidence; it does not select a model for outer evaluation, deployment, or physiological interpretation. A later selection-bearing comparison needs a newly frozen, denser schedule and explicit inner/outer grouped manifests, with gaze-condition groups spanning captures or whole-capture splits as appropriate. Use the same xy measurement contract and the declared no-absolute-accuracy-gates policy. Captures 5/6 remain outside this screen.

The 36 fits used CPU because the accommodation-family GPU value, derivative, branch, and certificate parity has not been verified. An outside-sandbox read-only probe found two visible GPUs (NVIDIA RTX PRO 2000 Blackwell and GeForce RTX 5070 Ti), with a minimal CuPy operation succeeding on device 0.
